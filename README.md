# Family Secretary

A local, privacy-first assistant for busy families, running on a Raspberry Pi at home.

Every morning and afternoon it gathers the day's weather, the family calendar and school schedules, works out what the children should wear and when they need to leave, and presents it all as a calm, readable picture. The first output channel is a Samsung Frame TV in Art Mode: the family's to-do list hangs on the wall as a piece of art. More channels will follow.

## Principles

- **Local first.** Data stays at home; nothing depends on a subscription.
- **Readable by everyone.** Large type and icons that a four-year-old can follow.
- **Configurable.** Each family sets its own people, colours, places and rules.

## Status

Working prototype in daily use since November 2026. Code will be published here once it has been tidied and stripped of private settings.

Tested with Samsung Frame Pro (2025). Other Frame models may need adjustments.
